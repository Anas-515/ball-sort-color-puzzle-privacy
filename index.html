Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Ball Sort Color Puzzle Game respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our game.

Important: Ball Sort Color Puzzle Game itself does not directly collect or store personal information. The game does not require users to create an account.

Information We Collect

Ball Sort Color Puzzle Game does not directly collect or store personal information from users.

The game does not require account registration. We do not directly request your name, email address, phone number, password, photos, contacts, messages, or other personal information.

The game uses the third-party Unity Ads SDK for advertising services.

Unity Ads may collect and share certain information through its advertising SDK. This data collection is performed by Unity Ads, not directly by the game developer.

Unity Ads Data Collection

According to Unity's official Google Play data-safety disclosure for Unity Ads SDK 4.21.0, the SDK may collect and share the following data:

Location

  • Approximate location — collected and shared.

Unity Ads does not collect precise location according to its SDK disclosure.

Personal Information

  • Personal identifiers — collected and shared.
  • Name — not collected.
  • Email address — not collected.
  • Address — not collected.
  • Phone number — not collected.

Financial Information

  • Purchase history — collected and shared.
  • Credit information — not collected.
  • Credit card information — not collected.
  • Debit card information — not collected.
  • Bank account numbers — not collected.
  • Other financial information — not collected.

App Activity

  • Ad-related page views and taps — collected and shared.
  • In-app search history — not collected.
  • Installed-app information — not collected.
  • Other user-generated content — not collected.

Unity states that page views and taps are collected during the advertising experience. They are not collected during normal gameplay.

Unity Ads may also collect app usage times when the Acquire Optimization feature is enabled in the Unity Monetization dashboard.

App Diagnostics

  • App diagnostics — collected and shared.
  • Crash logs — not collected.
  • Other app performance data — not collected.

Device Information

  • Device or other identifiers — collected and shared.

Other Data Not Collected

  • Health information
  • Fitness information
  • Emails
  • SMS or MMS messages
  • Other in-app messages
  • Photos
  • Videos
  • Voice recordings
  • Music files
  • Other audio files
  • Files and documents
  • Calendar events
  • Contacts
  • Web browsing history

Advertising

Ball Sort Color Puzzle Game uses Unity Ads to display advertisements.

Unity Ads may process advertising-related information for purposes including advertising, analytics, app functionality, fraud prevention, security, and compliance.
